Learn good kitchen habits

Small steps help make cooking easier and safer.

🧼

Keep it clean

Wash hands, utensils and work surfaces. Keep raw meat separate from ready-to-eat food.

🥄

Prepare first

Read the recipe before starting, measure ingredients and chop vegetables before heating the pan.

🌡️

Cook safely

Cook meat, fish and eggs thoroughly. Refrigerate leftovers promptly and reheat them thoroughly.
